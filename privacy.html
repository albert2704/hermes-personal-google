Data practices

Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy describes Albert’s personal, self-hosted Hermes Google integration. It applies to this integration, not to every Hermes installation or to the separate policies of Google, GitHub, AI providers, Telegram or Trello.

Google data accessed and used

Subject to the permissions the owner grants, the assistant may access Gmail message metadata and content, labels and drafts; Calendar calendars and event details; and, when enabled for requested tasks, Drive files, Docs documents, Sheets spreadsheets and Contacts. It may make changes such as organizing messages, sending requested emails, or managing events when instructed by the owner. Data is used to carry out those requested features, not to offer a service to other users.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on the owner’s computer with owner-only file permissions. The assistant does not collect the owner’s Google password. Task outputs, selected source content, logs, caches and backups can also be stored locally. Access tokens, refresh tokens and private account data are not published in this website’s repository.

AI processing and other connected services

Information relevant to an owner-requested task, including selected Google data, may be included in prompts sent to the configured AI provider. Processing and retention by that provider depend on its terms and the owner’s account settings; the owner should review those controls before processing private data. Requested reminders and task outputs can be sent to Telegram, Trello or other services the owner explicitly connects. This integration does not itself train machine-learning models.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. The integration’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Locally stored task records and backups remain until the owner removes them or an enabled retention rule removes them; there is no single universal deletion period. Copies delivered to other services are subject to those services’ retention settings. The owner can revoke Google access in Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not automatically delete previously stored local or third-party copies.

This public website

These documentation pages contain no Google account data, credentials, sign-in form, analytics script or tracking pixel. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process normal hosting request information under its own privacy statement.

Contact and updates

For questions or requests concerning this personal integration, contact albert04.dev@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if the integration’s actual data practices change.
