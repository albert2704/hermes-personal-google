Personal Google integration

Hermes (personal)

A self-hosted personal assistant operated by Albert for his own Google account. These pages document the integration; they do not offer public sign-up or access to anyone’s account.

What it does

The assistant helps its owner review Gmail, organize messages, read and manage Google Calendar events, prepare daily briefings, and deliver requested reminders. Sending messages and changing account data are performed only in response to the owner’s instructions.

The same installation can also use Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts for owner-requested tasks when the corresponding permissions and APIs are enabled. Google’s consent screen lists the permissions actually requested.

Why Google access is requested

Google OAuth allows the owner to approve account access without giving this integration a Google password. A locally stored refresh token lets the assistant continue authorized work between interactive sign-ins. The owner can revoke that authorization.

Data handling

The assistant runs on the owner’s computer. Relevant task content may be sent to a configured AI provider, and requested outputs may be delivered through connected services such as Telegram or Trello. This is not a promise that all Google data stays on the computer. See the privacy policy for details and the terms of use for the personal-use scope.
